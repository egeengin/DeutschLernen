# Diagnostic Mock Exam

---

## Instructions

This is a baseline assessment to identify your weak spots for the **telc Deutsch B1** exam.

1. Use a timer.
2. Do not use a dictionary.
3. Mark your score.

---

## 1. Sprachbausteine (Grammar/Vocab)

<div class="md-quiz"
  data-question="Ich bleibe zu Hause, ___ ich krank bin."
  data-options="dass|weil|wenn"
  data-answer="1"
  data-explain="Correct: 'weil' provides a reason, and the verb is at the end."></div>

<div class="md-quiz"
  data-question="Ich bin nicht sicher, ___ der Arzt heute Nachmittag Sprechstunde hat."
  data-options="obwohl|dass|ob"
  data-answer="2"
  data-explain="Correct: 'ob' (whether) is used for indirect questions."></div>

<div class="md-quiz"
  data-question="Ich warte ___ Bus."
  data-options="auf dem|auf den|auf der"
  data-answer="1"
  data-explain="Correct: 'warten auf' always takes Akkusativ. 'den Bus' is masculine Akkusativ."></div>

<div class="md-quiz"
  data-question="Er hat mir gesagt, ___ er morgen nicht kommen kann."
  data-options="weil|dass|ob"
  data-answer="1"
  data-explain="Correct: 'dass' introduces a reported statement. The verb 'kann' goes to the end."></div>

<div class="md-quiz"
  data-question="Ich möchte einen ___ Kaffee, bitte."
  data-options="heiß|heißen|heißer"
  data-answer="1"
  data-explain="Correct: After 'einen' (masc. Akkusativ), the adjective takes the '-en' ending."></div>

## 3. Schriftlicher Ausdruck (Writing Sample)

You received this email from your German course teacher:

> Liebe Kursteilnehmer, am Samstag machen wir einen Ausflug nach Heidelberg. Bitte bringen Sie etwas zu essen und zu trinken mit.

**Write a short reply (approx. 80 words). Address these points:**

- Thank the teacher for the invitation
- Say whether you can come
- Ask about the meeting time and place
- Suggest something to bring

---

## Results

- **5/7 or more correct:** You are on track for B1. Focus on speed.
- **3–4/7 correct:** Review grammar (Week 1 in your study plan).
- **Less than 3/7:** Start from the basics with the Grammar & Vocabulary module.
