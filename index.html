Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our exam sprint curriculum, AI letter grader, pricing, and offline access.

Is DeutschLernen officially affiliated with telc gGmbH or BAMF? +

DeutschLernen is an independent exam preparation platform. We strictly adhere to the official telc Deutsch B1 certification blueprint (180/300 passing threshold, 3-criteria letter rubric) and the official 310 BAMF question catalogue for Leben in Deutschland (LiD / Einbürgerungstest). We are not endorsed by or affiliated with telc gGmbH or Goethe-Institut.

How does the AI Letter Grader score practice submissions? +

The evaluator calculates scores using the authentic telc B1 formula: (Criterion I: Task Content + Criterion II: Communicative Register + Criterion III: Formal Grammar/Syntax) × 3 = Final Score / 45 points. It verifies fulfillment of all 4 required Leitpunkte and honors telc's 'Primat der Verständlichkeit' (Priority of Comprehensibility).

Are purchases recurring subscriptions with monthly automatic rebilling? +

No! All passes (Quick Diagnostic €4.99, Standard €29, Citizenship €39, Extended €49) are single, one-time payments. We do not automatically renew or charge your payment method after your exam preparation period ends.

Can I study completely offline without an internet connection? +

Yes. DeutschLernen is a Progressive Web App (PWA). All 2,000+ vocabulary words, audio pronunciation synthesis, grammar lessons, and 310 BAMF LiD questions are cached directly in your browser. You can install it on your iOS, Android, or desktop device and practice anywhere.
